## 1. Product purpose

Manage multiple upstream generation accounts, their health, and daily/monthly quoted-point budgets from one same-origin operational console.

## 2. Primary user

A single service operator working primarily on a desktop and occasionally on a phone, who needs to identify and resolve account, budget, and task problems quickly.

## 3. Three to five principles

1. **Main-flow safety beats saving a click.** Mutations that can strand active work require explicit consequences and confirmation.
2. **Budget is operational data, not decoration.** Wallet balance, configured limits, reserved usage, charged usage, and exhaustion must remain visually distinct.
3. **Dense on desktop, complete on mobile.** Compact tables may become cards, but narrow layouts never hide a core action.
4. **Status must survive without color.** Every state uses text or an icon in addition to restrained semantic color.
5. **Credentials never become client state.** Administrator and upstream secrets do not enter browser storage, URLs, logs, or rendered responses.

## 4. Success metric for the surface

Within 60 seconds, the operator can identify the account that needs action and complete a health check, edit, enable/disable action, or budget adjustment. The complete workflow remains usable without page-level horizontal overflow at 1440×900 and 390×844.

## 5. Out of scope

- Does not manage downstream users or downstream API keys.
- Does not implement roles, RBAC, OAuth, or multi-administrator collaboration.
- Does not add historical charts or analytics beyond current operational summaries and task lists.
- Does not provide theme switching.
- Does not expose speculative settings that the server cannot apply.
